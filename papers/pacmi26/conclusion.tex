\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Systems built on language model components must decide how much
verification to buy and where to spend it.
We introduce epistemic observability (a hierarchy of tiers, each exposing
richer epistemic information at greater cost) to identify the cost surface
for that decision, and build a low-tier tensor interface that reduces the
verification budget.
